The challenge

KFC has the best delivery channel… and almost nobody uses it.

74 M€

in delivery sales a year

€6–16M

The problem is not the product or the brand. It's about who owns the customer: today the platforms do, with their data and their algorithm.

Aggregators acquire. Own channels retain.

The plan in three parts

A role for each channel, a reason to come back.

TE1 · Channel strategy

A role for each platform

Glovo · defend volume and renegotiate commissions Uber Eats · accelerate as a partner: from 43% to 70% of restaurants and own the late-night slot Just Eat · fix delivery times and fleet before promoting

Golden rule: no platform above 45%.

TE2 · CRO and loyalty

15 actions and the #PolloPollo Club

Quick wins · moment-based CRM, an “Order direct” campaign and the QR card Three-tier club · Crispy, Original and Colonel, with an Eduardo pre-sale Goal · 1 million members in six months

A 5% reward costs less than 1%, compared with a 10–25% commission.

TE3 · Digital project · my part

Eduardo Drop: the excuse to come back

€3.99 · symbolic price and free shipping 5,000 units numbered Eduardo Beats 1 per person · only after ordering on KFC.es

The drop brings the customer in; the club keeps them.
