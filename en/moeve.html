Moeve: from oil company to sustainable mobility.

A digital roadmap so that Moeve, formerly Cepsa, can make its new positioning credible within its Positive Motion 2030 plan. Five blocks: business strategy, ethics and SDGs, digital marketing plan, open innovation and market research with Gen Z.

The offer is not the problem. People who know Moeve's mobility services rate them well. The problem is that hardly anyone knows them, and the oil-company past weighs on credibility.

Open innovation in a digital environment

Three horizons of innovation.

H1 · Incremental

Eco-friendly fuel and oil

Improve what it already sells with a less polluting alternative.

H2 · Adjacent

Light vehicle repairs

A new service that makes use of the time customers spend at the station.

H3 · Transformational

Exercise bikes that generate electricity

An idea that turns waiting into energy and into a story to tell.

It all rests on a complete mobility experience: app development, a loyalty programme and rest areas with better services. We validated it with a Lean Canvas.

Market research and social media listening

What does Gen Z think about Moeve's change?

Qualitative

Focus group

Five Gen Z participants from Zaragoza and Barcelona, to understand the whys.

Quantitative

Online survey

Four blocks of questions, one per hypothesis, from general to specific.

Digital

Social media listening

Search trends and social conversation about charging, electric mobility and sustainability.

Insight 1

Sustainable, but practical

Sustainability matters, but only if it comes with convenience and services to hand.

Insight 2

Charging is the big barrier

Few points, waiting and having to plan. An opportunity for Moeve's stations.

Insight 3

Greenwashing alert

The rebrand modernises the brand, but many still see it as an oil company.

Strategic key

Consistency and real actions, told on the channels where young people are.
