Why Display and RTB in a launch

The block's role in the plan.

Reach

Reach beyond social

Display extends the launch's coverage to media and websites gamers visit, beyond social platforms.

Precision

Buy impression by impression

With RTB each impression is bid on in real time based on audience and context, instead of buying fixed placements.

Conversion

Follow through to purchase

Programmatic lets you retarget people who have already shown interest and move them towards pre-ordering or buying on release day.
